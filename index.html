·How this is meant to be used

Each night you do three things, in order, and none of them takes long.

You count. Open Anki's stats, copy the Today block, paste it in below. The numbers fill themselves. They are not the point — they are the evidence.

You examine. Three questions, asked by Sextius every night of his life and kept by Seneca after him. Answer them plainly and about today only.

You write. Whatever the day left in you. No one else is going to read it.

Then the day is sealed and takes its colour on the calendar. Come back tomorrow and do it again. That is the whole method; there is no other.

If you are working toward something with a date on it, name it under The horizon on the Record page and every page will count down to it.

VThe journal

Marcus' book carried no title. What later editors called the Meditations he headed only to himself — it was never written to be read. Use this the same way. Whatever the day left in you, at whatever length, in whatever language it arrives in.
